Orblyx — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Orblyx ("the game") is developed by Brimzo Games. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we store

  • Game progress (scores, stars, skins, settings) is stored on your device.
  • Online features: to show rankings and battles, the game creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase and stores your chosen player name, scores, trophies, and recordings of your runs (the timing of your taps). No email, phone number or real name is collected.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address and usage data to serve and measure ads, and — where you consent — to personalise them. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice any time under Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, about the Daily Challenge). They are not sent from a server.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all on-device data. To delete your online rankings and recorded runs, email us with your player name and we will remove them.
